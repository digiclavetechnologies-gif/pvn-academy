Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens when you use the demo enquiry form on the Padmini Vidhya Niketan website.

Information you provide

The form asks for a student or parent name, phone number and class or course. The website does not submit these details to a website server or store them in a database.

WhatsApp enquiry

After you submit the form, the details are placed into a prefilled WhatsApp message. You must press Send in WhatsApp to share them with the institute. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its privacy policy applies to information shared through its service.

Questions

Contact Padmini Vidhya Niketan at +91 99110 00896 if you have questions about this policy.
