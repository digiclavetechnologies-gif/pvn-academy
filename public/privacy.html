Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens when you use the demo enquiry form on the Padmini Vidhya Niketan website.

Information you provide

The form asks for a student name, parent name, phone number, class, preferred batch, mode and an optional message. The website does not submit these details to a website server or store them in a database.

WhatsApp enquiry

After you submit the form and choose to continue, the details are placed into a prefilled message and opened in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is operated by a third party and its privacy policy applies to information shared through its service.

Questions
