Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how Padmini Vidhya Niketan (PVN Edu) handles information when you visit our website or contact us about classes. PVN Edu is a coaching institute in Laxmi Nagar and provides learning support, including CA Foundation study material; this policy covers information shared when visitors ask about those services. It also applies to details you choose to share with us by phone, email or WhatsApp. Please share only information needed to respond to your enquiry.

Information we collect for study material and coaching enquiries

The demo enquiry form asks for a student's name, a parent or guardian's name, phone number, class, preferred batch, study mode and an optional message. The form checks the details in your browser and opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. The website itself does not send form entries to a PVN web server or save them in a website database. If you contact us directly, we receive the details you choose to provide, such as your name, contact details and questions about classes.

When a page is requested, the website's hosting and security services may process basic technical request information, such as the requested page, IP address, browser type and time of the request, to deliver and protect the site. These technical records are separate from the demo enquiry form.

How we use information

PVN uses contact details you send us to answer questions, discuss class availability, arrange a demo or follow up on an enquiry. We do not use the website form to create student accounts or take payments. Please do not include passwords, identity documents, payment-card details, health information or other sensitive information in the optional message.

If a student is under 18, a parent or guardian should oversee the enquiry and any information shared. Please provide only what is needed to discuss the student's learning requirements.

WhatsApp and other third-party services

When you submit the demo form, your browser opens WhatsApp with the information entered in a pre-filled message. WhatsApp may process information in that link to open the message. The message is sent to PVN only if you choose to send it in WhatsApp. WhatsApp is a separate service; its privacy policy and settings apply to information you share there.

Our website also links to services such as Google Maps, YouTube, Instagram and the Student App. The homepage includes an embedded Google map. Opening an external service or loading its embedded content may allow that provider to receive technical information from your browser or use its own cookies and similar technologies. Those services operate under their own privacy notices, which we encourage you to review.

Cookies and analytics

The website does not currently use analytics or advertising tools that set tracking cookies. Your browser and hosting or embedded third-party services may handle technical data or cookies as part of loading their content. You can manage cookies through your browser settings; blocking them may affect how third-party features work. If our use of analytics or cookies changes, we will update this policy.

Data sharing

PVN does not sell personal information. We share information when you choose to send it through WhatsApp or another third-party service, when a service provider needs information to operate its service, or when disclosure is required by law. We do not use enquiry details for unrelated advertising. Information handled by another service is also subject to that provider's practices.

Data retention and security

The website does not retain demo form entries in a PVN website database. If you send an enquiry through WhatsApp, email or a phone call, PVN may keep the information needed to respond and manage the enquiry. We keep it only for as long as it is reasonably needed for that purpose or to meet applicable record-keeping requirements, then delete it when appropriate. WhatsApp and other providers may retain information under their own policies and account settings.

We take care when handling enquiries, but no website or messaging service can be guaranteed completely secure. Use a device and account you trust, and avoid sending sensitive details through the form or messaging services.

Your choices and requests

You may choose not to use the enquiry form and contact us by phone or email instead. You can ask PVN to provide, correct or delete personal information you have sent directly to us, or raise a concern about how it is used. We may need to confirm your identity before responding and may need to retain certain records where the law requires it. To manage information held by WhatsApp, Google or another provider, use that provider's privacy controls and request process.

Policy updates

We may update this policy when the website or our information practices change. The latest version will appear on this page. Please review it from time to time if you continue to use the website.
